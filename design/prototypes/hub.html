Recommendation

A hybrid: C's flow, A's map, B's proof

  1. C owns the shell and the flow. The phase rail decides which surface leads, and Decide is C's calm brief. The light theme comes from C.
  2. A owns the signature surface. The neural network is the live and explore view of a mission, and the project lens is the home overview of all work.
  3. B owns proof and replay. Revision gates and the cross-section become the Timeline lens and the Proof phase.
  4. One colour law everywhere: hue means who, red means stop, light means now, yellow means your turn.
  5. One type system: Archivo (its width axis gives condensed labels for dense maps and wide display headings from one family) with Atkinson Hyperlegible Mono for hashes, paths and numbers, where 0/O and 1/l/I must never be confused.

This keeps what only A does (the picture of relationships), what only B does (the exact-revision trust question) and what only C does (understandable without a tutorial, calm at delivery). Each part stays in the role it is strongest at, instead of being averaged.

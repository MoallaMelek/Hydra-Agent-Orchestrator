# Hydra Mission Control

Alpha.6 replaces the Workroom composition with an objective launch deck, compact command navigation and one stable orchestration scene. Operate mode: clarity of responsibility and evidence is the product's visual identity. A mission has six fixed stations: Plan, Build, Review, Repair, Engine checks and Your decision. Recorded transitions connect them; no force simulation, imaginary subtasks, invented thinking or continuous idle animation.

The scene stays still while its evidence changes. Role identities combine authored icon geometry, role label and provider name. The engine's checks are distinct from provider agents. Runs succeeding does not certify their output. Current verification/review trust uses exact revision/stage/spec bindings from the existing policy model. Earlier run selection hides the latest summary; unavailable historical verdicts/artifacts are explicit. Discrete replay uses recorded timestamps and projects outcomes only after their recorded completion. The same projection feeds canvas, records and swimlane timeline.

Selecting a station reveals the existing evidence inspector. Narrow windows bring that named inspector into view with immediate focus; Back to canvas restores the selected control. Manual selection does not get stolen by new updates. Native buttons, selects, ranges, disclosures, dialogs and tabs carry familiar keyboard semantics. Current plan/final decision controls sit in their relevant inspector; replay hides them. The mission dossier retains current base-to-head revision comparison, plan, findings, check commands, logs and durable receipts.

Midnight uses a graphite sea-green ground with a pale seafoam signal; Light uses warm paper with dark green ink; Terracotta uses warm mineral surfaces with a clay signal. Functional amber/red/green states retain their meanings and accompanying words. Role tints are never the sole identifier. CSS tokens are in `packages/ui/src/app/experience.css`. Platform typography is intentional for installed, cross-platform desktop consistency and offline reliability; no remote font or imagery dependency is introduced.

At desktop widths, mission canvas and inspector share one stable surface. At laptop widths the inspector stacks; at narrow widths the graph becomes a semantic participant list and the named chronological Records alternative remains available. The optional wide timeline has a scoped keyboard-focusable scroll region. No graph dragging or zoom is required. Fixed limits are six stations, 100 visible operation choices/records and 180 replay moments, with omissions labeled. Existing store/event limits remain intact.

Motion: short pointer feedback, no keyboard entrance delay, no constant telemetry motion. Auto follows system reduced motion; Reduced and Minimal remove movement/transitions without suppressing evidence. Replay is an explicitly started, stoppable series of recorded moments, not a simulated execution clock.

## Interaction review

| Before | After | Why |
|---|---|---|
| Pending rows before a tall configuration form | Project, objective and launch action together; setup disclosed once | Primary action fits the laptop viewport |
| Tall project sidebar and identical ledger pages | Compact command frame, searchable project history, account role/readiness layout and settings index | More working area and clearer screen purposes |
| Orchestration below a long report | Canvas and contextual inspector are the main work surface | Contributions and decisions become inspectable where they occur |
| Latest review summary remained beside an earlier run | Only the current review gets its summary; older snapshot absence is explicit | Avoids attaching an approval to an earlier objection |
| Narrow selection only changed a border | Immediate named-inspector focus and return-to-canvas control | Makes the result of selection discoverable |
| Apply replay could retain a completion-bearing label | Shared outcome-safe replay projection | Does not reveal an outcome before its recorded end |
| Plain paragraph received Markdown; usage omitted cache | Safe Markdown renderer and cache-inclusive reported totals | Readable content and truthful budget context |

The installed Impeccable, app-appropriate Taste redesign resource and Emil design-engineering guidance were consulted passively. Website-specific Taste v2 draw/imagery, executable skill loaders/hooks and unreviewed scripts were deliberately skipped. The skill matrix, prototype comparison, measured evidence and limitations are in `docs/v2/EXPERIENCE_REVOLUTION.md` and the accompanying delivery outputs. No novice study, screen-reader certification or commercial-readiness claim follows from these design decisions.

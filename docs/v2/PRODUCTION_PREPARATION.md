## Product concepts considered before implementation

Scores are design judgments, not user-study results (5 strongest; learning means easiest to learn).

| Concept | Originality | Comprehension | Feasibility | Performance | Accessibility | Identity | Learning | Daily impact |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| Workroom: conversation canvas + contextual proof inspector | 4 | 5 | 5 | 5 | 5 | 5 | 5 | 5 |
| Change dossiers: project-first change documents with attached discussion | 5 | 4 | 4 | 5 | 5 | 5 | 4 | 4 |
| Session itinerary: guided full-screen journey through each stage | 4 | 5 | 3 | 4 | 4 | 4 | 5 | 3 |

Chosen: Workroom. It fits quick questions and substantial changes without creating paperwork or hiding concurrent independent tasks. It preserves existing provider/worktree/evidence mechanisms. The signature Proofline is a semantic ordered sequence of recorded artifacts: plan, revision, review, checks, decision and delivery. It never invents thinking, progress percentages or simultaneous within-task collaboration. Each artifact opens existing technical inspection; no idle animation or separate canvas engine is introduced.

The project composer stays discoverable, the decision inbox is bounded initially, actual active work gets a compact visible row, and failed decisions show the primary recovery action before disclosed detail. Deep pine navigation and mineral/slate work surfaces retain existing contrast-tested semantics and three themes. New interaction uses native buttons, focus targets, headings and lists.

Safe passive skill selection: accessible Impeccable 4.6.0 (Operate/new-work/craft floor), Emil interaction guidance, current Taste frontend guidance. Impeccable executable context/runtime was deliberately not run under the user's safety instruction; PRODUCT/DESIGN/source and packaged screenshots were read directly. Taste website imagery/mass-motion prescriptions and redundant experimental styles were skipped. No skill hooks, binary downloads or unreviewed scripts were run.

## Measurable qualification scope

- Preserve and regression-test strict engine approvals across local/desktop/remote clients.
- Close fresh provider redaction/output, instance ownership, configured Git execution and remote response-bounds defects with adversarial disposable tests.
- Provide explicit task-content purge and consistent backup read-back validation without promising permanent media erasure or automatic installer restore.
- Measure identical generated 1k/10k/50k task workloads before/after query tuning; keep startup/paging bounded and search correct after edits/purge/reopen.
- Inspect real packaged UI in all themes/1360/900/390px sizes, proof navigation, approval/recovery/purge states and restart.
- One owner-approved cross-platform Actions qualification; actual artifacts/installers in disposable CI runners, never the personal installation.
- Owner-approved real-agent evaluation capped at $1 Claude and 50,000 reported Codex tokens; retain failures. No Grand Prix spend or benchmark ranking claims.
- Recheck official Agensi mechanisms; full app remains the objective. Prepare unsent partnership/commercial drafts, never an installer disguised as a skill.

## Boundaries

Docker is not required for Hydra and remains stopped: the owner questioned necessity rather than authorizing startup. This build continues to require trusted repositories. Git hardening, process cleanup, limited environments and check approval are not hostile-code containment. A container/native sandbox remains unqualified. Trusted remote-client delivery, publisher signing/entitlements, paid certificate accounts, actual physical-phone tests and failed-installer recovery require separate qualification/owner inputs. CI alone is not commercial readiness.

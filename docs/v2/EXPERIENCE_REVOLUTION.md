## Interaction decision

The three user images are references for relationship legibility, grouped participants and selectable evidence. They are not Hydra screenshots or evidence that Hydra implements their products' features. We use bounded directed relationships, not a security-dashboard costume, free-form force graph or simulated multi-agent concurrency.

Three original interactive prototypes are generated by `tools/experience-prototypes.mjs`. Each is a standalone local HTML study, clearly labeled as a fixture. Mission Control supports objective composition and team/decision inspection; Neural Canvas supports participant selection and discrete record inspection; Living Workspace switches the central artifact between phases. `tools/experience-prototype-check.mjs` renders all three in Electron 44.7.0 and exercises launch/review controls. These are interaction studies, not production-screen or user-study results.

## Acceptance criteria

- The project, objective and launch action are together in the first laptop/desktop viewport; no autonomy configuration is required before writing an objective.
- Orchestration is central in change missions. Six fixed stations distinguish plan, build, review, repair, engine checks and human decision with shape/icon/role/provider labels.
- Connections represent recorded stage transitions. Repair links appear only after a repair stage begins. A finished agent process never alone means verification passed.
- Selecting a station or operation exposes its existing evidence inspector. Revision diff, exact check commands, findings and delivery receipts remain available through named actions.
- Replay uses discrete recorded timestamps, bounded to 180 moments, with explicit omissions. Future completion/tool ticks and repair stages remain hidden. Current artifact summaries/output are unavailable in replay and clearly labeled as such.
- Three themes, 1360/900/390-width checks, native keyboard controls, visible focus, dialog Escape/focus, reduced motion and Minimal quality remain usable. No graph dragging or zoom is required.
- Existing engine approval, persistence, Git isolation and provider integrations remain intact. Local full checks/build and packaged Electron workflows must pass before delivery; skipped/unperformed coverage must be disclosed.
- Prepare before/after captures, 60–90-second truthful recorded demo, skill matrix, performance/accessibility results and a separate runnable preview; commit and verify push without main merge or publication.

### Critique and resulting corrections

A bounded read-only Codex reviewer inspected the concepts, source/captures and a running packaged fixture; the main chat alone wrote, integrated and tested changes. Earlier review records inherited the current approval summary, narrow selection did not reveal its inspector, and apply replay could disclose the future result. All three were corrected centrally. Final visual inspection also caught raw Markdown in the disclosed dossier and ancestor scrolling that clipped the command header; safe rendering and main-relative reveal corrected both. No unresolved reviewer finding is being hidden as polish. Commercial comprehension remains an expert judgment until novice usability testing.

Intermediate failures remain in local evidence: Light text contrast 4.343:1 (corrected), a keyboard driver that omitted the Enter character event (driver corrected after trusted keypress/click diagnosis), prototype hidden-paint capture flakiness (bounded retry), and the hidden-start invocation (corrected). Final passing reports supersede those attempts without erasing them.

### Performance observations and bounds

- Canvas layout is deterministic; no force simulation, graph physics, perpetual animation, idle replay timer or runtime image service. Replay is capped at 180 recorded moments and choices/lists at 100, with omissions disclosed. Existing engine transport/replay bounds remain.
- Final Home renderer consumed 0.019157 seconds of TaskDuration over a 10-second sample. This is a short Home sample, not whole-app CPU or battery qualification.
- One warm empty-profile retained-process-tree sample per build: alpha.5 ready 3743.79 ms, whole-machine CPU 0.14737%, working-set sum 475,774,976 bytes; alpha.6 ready 2124.48 ms, CPU 0.00613%, 477,519,872 bytes, each about 21 seconds. Shared pages are double-counted, exited children excluded, host load uncontrolled. These observations do **not** establish a statistically reliable startup/CPU/memory improvement or a long-soak claim.
- One simulated flood benchmark produced 4,068 events: 2212 ms wall time, 10.9 ms average frame interval, 278 ms maximum, two frames over 50 ms and one 79 ms long task. Reported heap delta 0 MB does not mean zero allocation. Smoothness under sustained live-provider load remains unqualified.
